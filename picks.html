Settled bets

Paper trading only: no real money is staked. Each bet is recorded at the odds on offer when the model first spots it, then settled from the final result. Settings re-run the same recorded bets under your rules, so they can only be tightened, never loosened past what the model recorded. Real bookmakers limit or ban winning accounts and prices move, so real-world results would be worse than shown. Changing settings after seeing results is a good way to fool yourself: judge the model on how it does over hundreds of bets.
